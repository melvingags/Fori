FAQ
Quick answers to common questions
How does FORI work for lawyers?
FORI connects you with clients who need legal help across 195 countries. Once you subscribe, your profile appears in the global lawyer directory. Clients can browse your specialisation, location, ratings, and bio — and if they're Premium users, they can send you a direct contact request. You review the request and accept or decline. If accepted, you communicate via the built-in secure chat.
How do I set up my profile to attract clients?
A strong profile includes: a clear specialisation (e.g. Corporate Law, Family Law), your city and country, a compelling bio that explains your experience and approach, your firm name, and your case count. The more complete your profile, the higher you rank in search results. Verified lawyers and boosted profiles appear at the top of the directory — both significantly increase visibility.
How do I get my Verified Badge?
The ✓ Verified badge signals to clients that you are a qualified, licensed legal professional reviewed by the FORI team. To get verified, go to your Profile section and click Get Verified, then submit your bar association certificate or practising licence. Our team reviews submissions within 48 hours. Verified lawyers appear above unverified lawyers in all search results — it's the single most impactful thing you can do for your visibility.
How do ratings and reviews work?
After a case is closed, clients are prompted to rate their experience with you (1–5 stars), select the case outcome (Won, Settled, Lost, Ongoing), and leave a written review. All ratings are verified by FORI — only clients who had an active case with you can leave a review. Your average rating and review count are displayed on your profile card. High ratings directly improve your ranking in the directory.
How do I receive and manage client requests?
When a Premium client sends you a contact request, it appears in your Client Leads section with their name, the nature of their legal matter, and the date received. You have 48 hours to Accept or Decline. If you accept, a secure chat opens between you and the client. If you decline or don't respond, the client is free to contact other lawyers. There's no limit on how many active cases you can manage simultaneously.
Can I close a case, and what happens when I do?
Yes. Either party can close a case at any time from the chat screen. Once closed, the chat becomes read-only and the client is prompted to leave a rating. Closed cases are archived in your case history. Closing a case cleanly and prompting a rating is important — positive reviews build your reputation on the platform over time.
Can clients share documents with me?
Yes. Inside the chat, clients can attach files (PDF, Word, images) directly to messages. You'll see the attachment inline and can open or download it. You can also send files to clients the same way. All file transfers are encrypted and stored securely — neither FORI staff nor third parties have access to case documents.
What does the Lawyer Subscription include?
Your Lawyer Subscription ($24.99/month or $19.99/month billed yearly) includes: a profile listing in the global FORI directory, unlimited client connection requests, the secure client chat system, case management tools, access to ratings and reviews, and eligibility for the Verified Badge. You also get priority support via live chat and email.
How do Profile Boosts work?
A Profile Boost temporarily pins your listing to the top of the lawyer directory and search results, above all unverified and unboosted profiles. This dramatically increases your visibility to clients browsing the directory. A 7-Day Boost costs $4.99 and a 30-Day Boost costs $12.99 — both are one-time purchases. Boosted profiles display a ⬆ Featured badge. You can purchase a boost from your Profile section at any time.
How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel anytime with no penalties. Your subscription is managed through Lemon Squeezy — the cancellation link is in your subscription confirmation email. Click Manage Subscription → Cancel Plan. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period, after which your profile is removed from the directory. Your account, case history, and ratings data are preserved.
Does FORI take a commission on cases?
No. FORI operates on a subscription model only — we never take a commission or percentage of any fees you charge your clients. What you earn from your clients is entirely yours. FORI's revenue comes solely from lawyer subscriptions, client Premium plans, Verified Badge purchases, and Profile Boosts.
Is client and case data kept confidential?
Absolutely. All case conversations, documents, and client information are encrypted and stored securely on Google Firebase. FORI staff cannot read your case chats. Client data is never shared with third parties, advertisers, or other lawyers. FORI is fully GDPR-aware and built with privacy as a core principle.
How do I get support if something goes wrong?
Use the Live Chat option at the top of this support panel for real-time help from our team. Alternatively, email support@lex-ai.com and we'll respond within 24 hours. For billing issues, include your account email and subscription ID. For technical bugs, describe what happened and we'll prioritise a fix.
