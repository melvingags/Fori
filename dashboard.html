FAQ
Quick answers to common questions
What is FORI and how does it work?
FORI is an AI-powered legal navigator available in 195 countries. You type a legal question, select your jurisdiction and area of law, and FORI gives you a clear, jurisdiction-aware answer in seconds. FORI also lets you upload documents for AI review, draft contracts, search the clause library, predict case outcomes, and connect directly with verified lawyers — all in one platform.
Is FORI's advice legally binding?
No. FORI provides legal information and general guidance — not formal legal advice. The information FORI provides is educational and designed to help you understand your situation, your rights, and your options. For binding legal decisions, court proceedings, or matters with significant financial or personal consequences, always consult a qualified lawyer. FORI makes it easy to find one in our directory.
Which countries does FORI cover?
FORI covers all 195 countries. When you ask a question, FORI tailors its answer to your selected jurisdiction — whether you're in Nigeria, the UK, the US, India, South Africa, or anywhere else. FORI auto-detects your country based on your timezone, but you can always change it manually from the dropdown in the chat.
What's the difference between Free and Premium?
The Free plan gives you 5 questions per day and access to the lawyer directory (view only). Premium ($4.99/month or $49.90/year) gives you unlimited questions, direct lawyer contact, document drafting, contract review, redlining, clause library, case outcome prediction, document comparison, legal research, and file uploads. Premium is billed securely through Lemon Squeezy and can be cancelled anytime.
How do I upgrade to Premium?
Click the Upgrade button in the sidebar or tap any Premium-gated feature. Choose monthly ($4.99/mo) or yearly ($49.90/yr — equivalent to 2 months free). You'll be taken to a secure checkout page. Once payment is confirmed, your account upgrades instantly and you can start using all Premium features immediately.
How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel anytime — there are no cancellation fees or penalties. To cancel, go to your Lemon Squeezy customer portal (the link is in your subscription confirmation email) and click Cancel Plan. Your Premium access continues until the end of your current billing period, after which your account reverts to Free. Your history and data are preserved.
What payment methods are accepted?
FORI accepts all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) via Lemon Squeezy, our secure payment partner. Lemon Squeezy is PCI-DSS compliant and your card details are never stored on FORI's servers. Additional payment methods may be added in future, including crypto and regional options for African users.
How do I contact a lawyer on FORI?
Go to Find a Lawyer in the sidebar. You can browse or search by name, city, country, or specialisation. Once you find a lawyer you'd like to work with, click Contact Lawyer (Premium required), describe your legal matter briefly, and send your request. The lawyer will receive it and accept or decline within 48 hours. If accepted, you can message each other directly inside FORI.
My lawyer hasn't responded — what do I do?
Lawyers have 48 hours to accept or decline a contact request. If there's no response after 48 hours, we recommend sending a contact request to a different lawyer from the directory — there's no limit on how many you can reach out to. If a lawyer declines, their reason isn't shared, but you're free to contact others. Your case history and description remain private.
How are lawyers verified on FORI?
Lawyers on FORI who display the ✓ Verified badge have been reviewed and verified by the FORI team. Verification confirms they are qualified, licensed practitioners. Unverified lawyers are still real professionals who have registered on the platform — they simply haven't yet completed the verification process. We recommend prioritising verified lawyers for sensitive or complex matters.
What can I use the AI tools for?
Premium users get access to six AI-powered tools: Contract Review (upload a contract and get a full risk analysis), Redline & Negotiate (get suggested edits and negotiation points), Clause Library (search and copy any legal clause), Case Outcome Prediction (describe your situation and get a likely outcome assessment), Compare Documents (compare two contracts side by side), and Legal Research (search legislation, case law and legal precedents with citations).
What file types can I upload?
FORI supports PDF, Word (.docx), and plain text (.txt) files for contract review, redlining, and document comparison. For the Documents vault, you can also upload images (PNG, JPG), Excel files (.xlsx), and other common formats. The maximum file size per upload is 10MB. Files are stored securely in your personal vault and can be accessed or deleted at any time from the Documents section.
Is my data safe and private?
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest, stored securely on Google Firebase — one of the world's most trusted cloud platforms. FORI never sells your personal data, never shares your questions or documents with third parties, and never uses your data to train AI models without consent. Your conversations, documents, and case history are private to your account only.
Can I delete my account and data?
Yes, at any time. Go to Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, case history, documents, connections, and all associated data from our servers. This action cannot be undone. If you're a Premium subscriber, please cancel your subscription first to avoid future charges before deleting your account.
