FAQ
Quick answers to common questions
How do I upgrade to Premium?
Click the Upgrade button in the top bar or sidebar. Premium unlocks unlimited questions, document drafting and lawyer connect for $4.99/month.
My lawyer hasn't responded — what do I do?
Lawyers have 48 hours to accept or decline a request. If there's no response, you can withdraw the request and contact a different lawyer from the directory.
Is LEX's advice legally binding?
No. LEX provides legal information and guidance, not formal legal advice. For binding legal matters or court proceedings, always consult a qualified lawyer.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to Settings → Subscription → Cancel Plan. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.
Is my data safe and private?
Yes. All data is encrypted and stored securely on Firebase. We never sell your personal data or share it with third parties. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
